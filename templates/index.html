What do these statuses and numbers mean?
  • Online An ICMP echo reply came back.
  • No reply No reply arrived in time. The host may be offline, or a firewall may be blocking ping while the host works normally.
  • DNS failed The hostname could not be turned into an IP address, so no ping was sent.
  • Check error The ping command itself could not run.
  • Reply rate = checks that got a reply ÷ checks recorded in the last 24 hours. Only checks that actually ran are counted: time when this app was closed, the Mac was asleep, or the host was paused is not included.
